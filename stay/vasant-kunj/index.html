Vasant Kunj, New Delhi: a short guide for guests

Vasant Kunj is a residential neighbourhood in south-west Delhi, with tree-lined sectors, big shopping malls and some of Delhi's oldest monuments close by.

Getting here

Indira Gandhi International Airport is also in south-west Delhi, so Vasant Kunj is a short drive from the airport. App-based taxis are the simplest way to get around.

Shopping and food

Three large malls stand side by side on Nelson Mandela Marg in Vasant Kunj: DLF Promenade, DLF Emporio and Ambience Mall, with restaurants, cafés, cinemas and shops.

Things to see nearby

  • Qutub Minar in neighbouring Mehrauli, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
  • Mehrauli Archaeological Park, with ruins and tombs from several centuries of Delhi's history.
  • Sanjay Van, a large city forest with walking trails.

Check opening times and entry rules on the official websites before you go.
